Ancestral Roots

The Sacred Origins of Kemetic Yoga

Kemetic Yoga is the ancient Egyptian system of yoga based on postures depicted on temple walls and hieroglyphs of Ancient Kemet (Egypt). It is a healing practice focused on spinal alignment, deep abdominal breathing, energy flow, and meditative self-discovery.

Ankh & Spinal Alignment

Geometric Postures

Emphasizes precise anatomical alignment modeled after the sacred geometry of Kemetic deities. Postures naturally realign the vertebrae and decompress spinal nerves.

Life Force & Breath

The Sesh Flow Method

Rule of Four breathing — inhalation, retention, exhalation, retention — activates the parasympathetic nervous system to release deep-seated stress and restore vitality.

Nile Valley Heritage

Ancestral Wisdom

Reconnects modern practitioners with ancient African healing philosophy. A gentle, restorative flow suitable for all ages, body types, and experience levels.
