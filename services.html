Frequently Asked Questions

Kemetic Yoga is based on the ancient Egyptian (Kemetian) system of renewal. It focuses heavily on slow, geometric postures, spinal alignment, and rhythmic rule-of-four breathing to channel energy safely through the physical and subtle body.

No prior experience is necessary. All sessions are structured to accommodate beginners while offering gentle progressions for returning practitioners.

Please wear comfortable, breathable clothing. We recommend bringing a personal yoga mat and a reusable water bottle. Props and mats are also available on site if needed.
